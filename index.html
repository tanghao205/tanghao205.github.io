AI TOOLS & ENGINEERING HARNESSES

A method for reading before building.

I use AI tools to work through documents, ticket descriptions, feature requirements, and bug reports while keeping the source in view.

My reusable reading method gives me a short map first, then more detail only where a decision needs it. In a particular project, I connect that analysis to its specifications, code review, and checks.

REUSABLE READING METHOD

Summarize the goal, expected behavior, constraints, assumptions, and open questions. Expand one unclear or high-risk point at a time and trace the answer to its source.

PROJECT WORKFLOW

Carry those decisions into scoped work and review. Separate guidance for AI tools from checks that actually verify a change.

A READING PATH FOR HUMANS

See the shape first. Choose where to go deeper.

A long source becomes three levels of detail. The reader chooses a point to expand; each level stays connected to the original material.

START WITH SOURCE MATERIAL

Document · ticket description · feature request · bug report

  1. LEVEL 1 Fast human scan
    What is this about?

    Up to ten short points surface the goal, scope, expected behavior, constraints, and open questions. You can scan the whole issue without reading every line.

    Browse the overview+

    A first pass contains up to ten points, each under 30 words. I look for the purpose, constraints, missing information, and which question deserves attention.

    Human choice: select one point that needs attention.

  2. LEVEL 2 Browse one point
    Why does it matter?

    Expand only the selected point in about a paragraph: its context, impact, assumptions, and evidence. Browse the detail without losing the whole picture.

    Open a focused explanation+

    The chosen point expands to about 100 words. I can inspect its context and impact, follow the source, or choose another point from the overview.

    Human choice: stop here or ask for exact detail.

  3. LEVEL 3 Deep dive on demand
    What must we verify?

    When needed, examine exact requirements, dependencies, edge cases, affected behavior, acceptance criteria, and unresolved questions for that point, with claims traced back to sources.

    Inspect the exact details+

    Only on request, I examine the selected point through requirements, dependencies, risks, impact, acceptance criteria, and next actions. Unknowns remain explicit.

    Result: a focused basis for a decision or further investigation.

THEN APPLY IT TO THE WORK

Feature: define the behavior, scope, impact, and acceptance criteria. Bug: compare expected and observed behavior, then investigate what the evidence supports.

In a project, I carry the result into its specification, review, and verification steps. AI helps organize the reading; I decide what the evidence supports.
